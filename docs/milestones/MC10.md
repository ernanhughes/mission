## Honest limitations

- Worker's `direct` executor is lower-parity and does not produce Work
  Evidence/Acceptance.
- The experiment is a bounded TraceLite proxy, not the full paper mechanism.
- Generated Python runs in a dedicated repository/test subprocess, not a hard
  OS sandbox.
- CoCoder review is independent but does not prove the research hypothesis.
- Work write-back, Writer, Movies and conversational autonomy remain out of scope.
