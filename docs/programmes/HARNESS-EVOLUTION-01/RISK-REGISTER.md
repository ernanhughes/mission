| ID | Risk | Level | Evidence / mitigation for later stages |
|---|---|---|---|
| K-01 | The same concepts have overlapping names but different authority: Worker Work/Acceptance, Mission Work Outcome, Praxis effectiveness, Writer ECK evaluation. | High | Crosswalk existing contracts before creating DTOs; keep declared acceptance separate from independent effectiveness. |
| K-02 | Existing uncommitted work is mistaken for stable/current capability or overwritten. | High | Snapshot all repository heads/status; restrict writes to this programme directory; compare status at exit. |

| K-07 | Current model/runtime cost is reported as a known invoice when only tokens or estimated prices are available. | Medium | Preserve Worker distinctions between billed, estimated, and unknown cost. |
| K-08 | Praxis's bounded research results are generalized beyond sample size/task families. | High | Preserve negative findings and insufficient-evidence verdicts; require representative held-out tasks. |
