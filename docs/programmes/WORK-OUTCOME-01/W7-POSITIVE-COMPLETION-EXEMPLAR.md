§16 asked for a genuine bounded exemplar with pre-authorized
requirements and independent assessment. Delivered: the 08C push
recovery.

1. **Objective**: resolve 08C merge-corroborated finding f7089844
   (push unproven at the claimed remote).
2. **Authorized requirements**: pre-existing accepted_proposal 08C
   dimension requirement (W6A) + proposal wp:75beaecfac034332 approved
   by w7-human for bounded read-only git checks (no repo changes).
3. **Execution**: deterministic remote-containment checks only.
4. **Outcome**: de28820 (linked to 08C) ∈ main and ∈ origin/main at
   origin https://github.com/ernanhughes/ai.git — push proven.
5. **Evidence**: merge-base + branch -r --contains outputs, remote
   URL, all cited in the closure record.
6. **Independent verification**: the checks are mechanical and
   re-runnable by anyone; the approval (human) and the evidence
   (git) come from different parties — no self-verification.
7. **Verified resolution**: finding resolved with cited evidence;
   proposal queued→(awaiting reported completion of any follow-up);
   history preserved at every step.

What this does and does not prove: it proves the full
finding → proposal → approval → handoff → verification → closure
lifecycle on real data with a real close. It does not prove an entire
programme complete (rightly — one finding ≠ one episode ≠ one
programme; §16's anti-gaming rules held). The unconditional
episode-level verified-complete exemplar remains open, now with a
working closure mechanism waiting for it.
